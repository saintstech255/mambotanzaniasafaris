Our aim is to help visitors experience these places through thoughtfully planned safari and travel experiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good to Know Before You Travel

How do I book a safari?

Contact us through WhatsApp. Tell us your preferred destination, travel dates and group size. Our team will discuss the options with you.

What do your packages cost?

Prices depend on the season, group size, accommodation and inclusions. Contact us for a quote based on your dates.

Can I change the duration of a package?

Yes. The durations shown are suggestions and can be adjusted according to your travel plans.

When is the best time to visit Tanzania?

Tanzania offers wildlife and adventure experiences throughout the year. The best period depends on the destination and activity.

Do you welcome domestic travellers?

Yes. We welcome travellers from Tanzania, East Africa and around the world.
